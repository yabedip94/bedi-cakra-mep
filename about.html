Frequently Asked Question

You May Ask

What scope of work does an integrated MEP contractor cover?
An integrated MEP contractor oversees the complete lifecycle of Mechanical (HVAC), Electrical (power and lighting), and Plumbing (water, drainage, fire fighting) systems, ensuring spatial coordination and seamless inter-system communication.
How do you approach energy efficiency in HVAC system design?
We implement variable speed drives (VFDs), high-efficiency chillers, smart Building Management Systems (BMS), and optimized air distribution to reduce thermal loss and minimize ongoing facility operational costs.
Which codes and standards govern your MEP installations?
Our engineering designs strictly comply with international standards including ASHRAE for HVAC, NFPA for fire life safety, IEC/IEEE for electrical networks, as well as Indonesian national codes (SNI) and local regulations.
What is included in the Testing and Commissioning (T&C) phase?
T&C includes pressure testing of pipework, insulation resistance testing for electrical gear, air/hydronic balancing (TAB), functional safety interlocking, and integrated fire scenario testing.
Can CAKRA MEP assist with retrofitting existing building systems?
Yes, we perform engineering condition audits, energy assessments, and phased equipment replacements to modernize aging utility infrastructure with minimal interruption to daily tenant operations.
How is clash detection managed before site installation?
We utilize coordinated 3D MEP modeling and BIM workflows to identify spatial clashes between ductwork, piping, cable trays, and structural elements well before physical fabrication and site delivery.
What electrical backup power solutions do you install?
We design and install automatic transfer switches (ATS), paralleling diesel generators, uninterrupted power supplies (UPS), and dedicated emergency lighting circuits for critical continuous power.
How do you ensure water quality and pressure in high-rise plumbing?
We design zoned variable-frequency booster pump systems, break tanks, pressure-reducing stations, and hygienic filtration units to maintain balanced, safe potable water pressure across all vertical floors.
What types of fire suppression systems do you deploy?
Depending on the space requirement, we deploy automatic pre-action and wet sprinkler systems, high-pressure foam systems, and clean agent gas systems (FM-200 and Novec) for server and electrical rooms.
Do you provide maintenance and post-handover service?
Yes, we offer scheduled preventive maintenance packages, thermographic inspections, consumable filter replacements, and 24/7 on-call engineering support to sustain optimal system longevity.
